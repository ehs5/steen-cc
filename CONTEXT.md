# steen.cc

Espen Steen's personal website. The same content is shown through several interchangeable designs that the visitor can switch between instantly.

## Language

**Content**:
Everything the site says: name, photo, location, birth year, about text, projects, contact and links. Defined once and shared by every design.
_Avoid_: Data, copy

**About text**:
The paragraphs that introduce Espen, shown directly under his name.
_Avoid_: Bio, intro, tagline

**Project**:
Something Espen has built, shown in the projects list with a name, a kind, a description, and optionally a link and a status.

**Kind**:
A short label saying what sort of thing a project is, such as "Company" or "CLI".
_Avoid_: Type, category, tag

**Status**:
A short label for a project that isn't fully available yet, such as "Under development".

**Design**:
One complete look for the site: layout, typography and colour. Every design renders the same content.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, template

**Draft**:
The approved mockup a Design is built from. A Design should match its Draft visually, changed only where the Content differs.
_Avoid_: Mockup, artifact, prototype

**Default design**:
The design shown to a visitor who hasn't chosen one.

**Switcher**:
The control that lets the visitor pick a design. It looks the same size and sits in the same place in every design.
_Avoid_: Toggle, selector, picker

**Mode**:
Light or dark variant of a design.
_Avoid_: Theme, colour scheme
